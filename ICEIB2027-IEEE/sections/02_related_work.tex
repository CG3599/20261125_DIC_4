\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Travel Time Prediction}
Graph neural networks have been applied to navigation ETA prediction \cite{eta}. Motorcycle speed models for rural roads also motivate the inclusion of road geometry in personalized pace prediction \cite{motorcycle}. The proposed system focuses on a single rider's historical trips and conservative trip-level arrival estimates.
\subsection{Language Agents and Verification}
TravelPlanner demonstrates the difficulty of satisfying multiple constraints using language agents \cite{travelplanner}. External critics and formal verification provide alternative approaches to improving planning reliability \cite{modulo,formal}. This work applies the separation of planning and verification to continuous-time motorcycle touring constraints.
