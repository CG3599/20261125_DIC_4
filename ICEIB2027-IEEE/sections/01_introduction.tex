\section{Introduction}
Mountain touring in Taiwan involves winding roads, substantial elevation changes, time-dependent weather, road closures, and limited fuel services. Navigation estimates based on aggregate traffic patterns may not capture the pace of an individual motorcyclist \cite{eta}. A feasible route at departure may become inaccessible by the time the rider arrives. This motivates a pre-trip planning system that jointly considers personalized arrival times and environmental constraints rather than providing a single estimated time of arrival (ETA).

The proposed contributions are (1) personalized segment-level travel-time prediction with uncertainty, (2) arrival-time-aware weather, road and fuel queries, and (3) a separation of language-model planning from deterministic feasibility verification. The system supports decisions before departure and does not predict crashes or guarantee riding safety.
